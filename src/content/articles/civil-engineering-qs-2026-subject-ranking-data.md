The scope of this article is global. The subject table lists 277 institutions. The country leaderboard reflects the number of ranked institutions per country as parsed from the subject table. The rank bands and score bands are derived from the same source. No median rank is given for this subject, because the institution at the median position has only a published rank range (101-150).

There are several limitations to note. First, the country leaderboard only shows the top ten countries by ranked institution count; countries with fewer ranked programs are not displayed. Second, the rank bands are based on the integer floor of the rank, so tied ranks may produce counts that do not sum exactly to the total. Third, the score bands are aggregated and do not show individual institution scores. Fourth, the data does not include unranked institutions, so the total number of programs worldwide in this subject is likely larger than 277. Fifth, the source does not provide country-level rank distributions, so the quality distribution within each country cannot be assessed from this data. Finally, the rankings reflect the methodology and data available as of the benchmark date; they are a snapshot, not a permanent assessment.
