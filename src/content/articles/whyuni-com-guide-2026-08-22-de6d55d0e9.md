## A Decade of Rankings Data

Over the past ten years, four Australian universities have shown remarkable stability across major global rankings. This stability is evident when examining their positions in the QS World University Rankings, the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings, the Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU), and the U.S. News Best Global Universities rankings.

## The Four Stable Universities

These four institutions have consistently maintained high positions, resisting the annual fluctuations that affect many other universities. Their performance across the four key ranking systems has been notably consistent, indicating a strong and sustained academic reputation.

## How the Rankings Are Evaluated

To assess stability, rankings from QS, THE, ARWU, and U.S. News are aggregated and compared. The composite score used in this analysis is calculated as an arithmetic average based on the public methodologies of these ranking bodies, without any additional weighting. This approach provides a transparent and unbiased view of each university's standing.

## Why This Matters

For university decision-makers and students, choosing a university with a stable ranking history can provide assurance about academic quality and global recognition. The four universities identified here have proven their consistency over a decade, making them reliable choices for those seeking a stable academic environment.
