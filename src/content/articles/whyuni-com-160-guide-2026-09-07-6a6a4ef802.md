## Introduction

If you are planning to study in New Zealand with the goal of migrating through the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC), understanding how to reach the 160-point threshold is crucial. The points system rewards qualifications in certain fields, and aligning your major with this system can increase your chances of meeting the required score.

## How the Points System Works

The SMC points system awards points for factors such as age, skilled employment, and qualifications. Points for qualifications vary depending on the level of study, but the field of study can also influence eligibility. To reach 160 points, you need to combine these factors strategically.

## Study Majors That Can Help Reach 160 Points

Certain professions are in high demand in New Zealand, and obtaining a qualification in such fields can strengthen your points profile. While the list of occupations that qualify for skilled migration changes, fields like engineering, IT, and healthcare are typically emphasized. For example, degrees in information technology, civil engineering, and nursing are often recognized.

## Choosing a University and Major

When selecting a university, consider the quality of the program and whether it is recognized by New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA). A qualification that is not recognized may not earn points. Also, look for opportunities for internships or work placements, as skilled employment is a high-value factor in the points system.

## Other Factors to Boost Your Score

Beyond your major, improving other points factors can help you reach 160. These include gaining skilled employment in New Zealand, which awards significant points, and being under a certain age. Planning your career path and securing a job after graduation can be as important as your choice of major.

## Conclusion

Reaching the 160-point threshold for New Zealand Skilled Migration requires a combination of a qualification in an in-demand field and other strategic choices. By researching the points system and aligning your major with high-demand occupations, you can improve your migration prospects. Remember to check current immigration policies, as they are subject to change.
