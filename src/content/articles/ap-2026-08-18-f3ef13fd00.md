## Boarding vs Day School: Key Differences
When choosing a US private high school, families weigh the benefits of boarding versus day attendance. Boarding schools provide a residential experience, while day schools offer the opportunity to live at home. Each model has distinct implications for daily routine and family involvement.

## AP Course Offerings
AP course availability varies between schools. Some boarding schools offer a wide range of AP subjects, while day schools may have different strengths. When comparing, examine the specific AP courses each school provides and how they match your academic goals.

## Extracurricular Activities and Resources
Extracurricular opportunities also differ. Boarding schools often have extensive after-hours activities due to their residential nature, while day schools may offer robust programs tied to the local community. Review each school's clubs, sports, and arts offerings to see which environment better supports your interests.

## Making the Choice
For low-age study-abroad families, the decision involves weighing academic offerings, extracurricular resources, and the living arrangement. Visit schools, talk to current students, and consider your child's independence and support needs before deciding.
