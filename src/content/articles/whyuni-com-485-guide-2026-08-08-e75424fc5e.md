## What is the 485 second post-study work visa?

The 485 Temporary Graduate visa allows international students to stay in Australia after completing their studies. The second post-study work stream provides an additional period of stay for those who have studied and lived in a regional area. The length of the second visa depends on where you studied and lived.

## Eligibility requirements for the 485 second visa

To be eligible, you must hold a first 485 visa and have lived in a regional area while studying. The second visa is generally available to those who obtained their first 485 visa after studying in a regional Australia location.

## How long can you stay with a second 485 visa?

The additional stay period usually matches the time you spent living and studying in regional Australia, with a maximum of two years. The exact duration depends on the regional classification of your study location.

## How to use the second 485 visa to gain work experience

Once granted a second 485 visa, you can live and work anywhere in Australia, but staying in a regional area can help you meet regional workforce needs. Use the extra time to gain professional experience in your field, which may strengthen your future career or visa pathway.

For detailed guidance on regional study and visa pathways, consider seeking advice from a registered education provider or migration professional.
