## Funding Requirements 2026

The UK student visa for 2026 has specific funding requirements you must meet. You need to show you have enough money to cover your tuition fees and living costs. The exact amount depends on your course and where you will study. For courses in London, you need to show more funds than for courses outside London. The funds must be held in a bank account for at least 28 days before you apply.

## Tuberculosis Test: What's Changed

If you are from a country where you must take a tuberculosis test, you need to provide a valid TB test certificate when you apply. The test must be from a clinic approved by the UK Home Office. The certificate is usually valid for six months, so ensure it is still valid on the day you submit your application. Any changes to the test requirements should be checked with the official guidance.

## Document Checklist

Before you submit your visa application, gather these essential documents:
- Your passport
- A recent photograph
- Your tuberculosis test certificate (if applicable)
- Proof of financial funds (bank statements or sponsor letters)
- Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your university
- Academic qualifications and English language test results (if required)
- Any other documents specified in your CAS

Make sure all documents are in the correct format and language. If any documents are not in English, you need a certified translation.

## Additional Tips

Double-check all requirements with the official UK government website before applying. The rules can change, so it's important to verify the latest information. Also, ensure your bank statements are recent and meet the 28-day requirement. If you have any doubts, consult your university's international student office.
