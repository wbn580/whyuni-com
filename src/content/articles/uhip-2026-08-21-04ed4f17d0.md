## What UHIP Covers

The University Health Insurance Plan (UHIP) is a mandatory health insurance plan for international students at participating Ontario universities. During the coverage period, UHIP provides access to medically necessary services such as doctor visits, hospital care, laboratory and diagnostic tests, and emergency medical services. This helps protect students from high medical costs that can arise without insurance.

## How to Use UHIP for Medical Care

To receive health services, you should present your UHIP card or proof of coverage at the time of care. For non-emergency visits, you can typically visit a walk-in clinic or a physician's office. For emergency situations, go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Always carry your UHIP card with you to ensure smooth processing.

## Reimbursement and Claims

If you pay for a medical service upfront, you may need to submit a claim for reimbursement. Keep all receipts and original documents, and follow the claim process outlined by your university's health plan administrator. Note that not all services may be covered, and some services may require pre-approval. To avoid unexpected costs, verify coverage before receiving non-emergency care.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to enroll in UHIP?

Yes, UHIP is mandatory for international students at participating Ontario universities, and you are automatically enrolled unless you have comparable coverage that qualifies for an exemption.

### What should I do if I need a specialist?

For specialist care, you will usually need a referral from a primary care physician. The specialist visit may be covered under UHIP, but confirm the referral and coverage details in advance.

### How do I avoid high medical bills?

Always present your UHIP card when receiving care, use UHIP-covered facilities, and seek pre-approval for non-emergency procedures. Keep all paperwork related to your claims.
