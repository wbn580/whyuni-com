## Types of On-Campus Accommodation
Australian universities offer a range of on-campus housing, from traditional dormitories to self-contained apartments. Common options include single rooms, shared rooms, and studio apartments. Some residences have shared bathrooms and kitchens, while others offer private ensuite facilities and full kitchens.

## Facilities and Amenities
Most residential colleges provide communal areas such as lounges, study spaces, and recreational facilities. Many include meals in the accommodation contract, while others have self-catering options with access to communal kitchens. Internet access, laundry facilities, and 24-hour security are also standard in many residences.

## Price Ranges
Costs vary widely depending on the room type, meal plan, and location. Sharing a room is generally the most affordable option, while single rooms with ensuite bathrooms are at the higher end. Some residences include utilities and meals in the rent, while others charge extras for these services. It's important to compare what is included in each price.

## Application Deadlines and Early Application
Applications for on-campus accommodation typically have deadlines that fall before the start of the academic year. Many universities have limited spots, and popular residences fill up quickly. To secure a place, it is advisable to submit your application as early as possible. Some universities operate on a first-come, first-served basis, so missing the deadline could mean being placed on a waiting list or needing to find off-campus housing.

## Key Considerations
When choosing accommodation, consider your budget, preferred room type, and the facilities that matter most to you. Check whether meals are included and if there are additional fees. Also, note the application deadline and ensure you complete all required forms and payments on time. Applying early significantly improves your chances of getting your preferred accommodation.

## Summary
On-campus housing in Australia offers a variety of room types and facilities at different price points. Deadlines are crucial, and early application is key to avoiding disappointment. Be sure to research your options thoroughly and act promptly to secure a spot.
