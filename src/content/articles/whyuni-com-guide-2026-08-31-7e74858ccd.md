## Overview of 2026 Australian Government Scholarships

For research masters applicants, the Australian government offers several scholarship programs that are open for applications in 2026. These include the Endeavour Scholarships and the Research Training Program. Each program has its own specific application conditions and key timeframes.

## Endeavour Scholarships

The Endeavour Scholarships are a government-funded initiative that supports international students undertaking study or research in Australia. They are open for applications in 2026 and provide financial assistance for research masters programs. Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria set by the government, which typically include academic merit and a confirmed offer of admission to a participating Australian institution.

## Research Training Program

The Research Training Program (RTP) is another government-funded scholarship that supports research higher degree students, including those enrolled in research masters. In 2026, RTP scholarships are available to eligible applicants. The program provides funding to offset tuition fees and may also include a living allowance. Applicants should check the specific conditions and deadlines set by their chosen university, as application processes can vary.

## Application Conditions and Key Timeframes

To apply for these scholarships, you need to prepare a range of documents and meet the application conditions. Conditions may include previous academic transcripts, research proposal, and proof of English proficiency. It is essential to note the key timeframes for each program, as they differ. Some scholarships have deadlines as early as mid-year, while others may close later. Prospective applicants are advised to verify the exact deadlines with the relevant government department or through the scholarship official website.

## What to Consider

If you are planning to apply for a research masters in Australia, consider these scholarships as a potential funding source. You may also explore other options such as university-specific scholarships. However, the government scholarships mentioned above are separate from institutional offerings and require attention to their own conditions. Remember to check each program's official guidelines to ensure you have the most current information.
