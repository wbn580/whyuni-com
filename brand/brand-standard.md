# WhyUni brand standard

Status: homepage preview only, not deployed  
Position: university reasons, course comparison and decision support that leads into an application

## Core idea

WhyUni is a decision journal, not a ranking leaderboard. The visual system pairs an editorial folio with a side-by-side comparison sheet. It should feel considered, candid and useful at the moment an applicant must explain why one option fits better than another.

## Voice

- Lead with the decision and the evidence behind it.
- Explain trade-offs without manufacturing certainty.
- Use calm, specific language: course structure, location, cost, outcomes and personal fit.
- Current institution requirements remain authoritative.
- Never imply university, government or ranking-body endorsement.
- Never promise admission, scholarships, credit, visas or outcomes.

## Visual system

- Primary: deep plum `#3c173d`
- Secondary: editorial plum `#5f2d60`
- Action: peach `#f0a17d`
- Supporting field: lavender `#d8c9e6`
- Reading surfaces: cream `#f8f2e9` and paper `#fffaf3`
- Display type: Georgia or a compatible editorial serif
- Utility type: Inter or a compatible neutral sans serif
- Corners remain square; hierarchy comes from paper, rules, scale and spacing.

## Conversion contract

The primary action is “Talk through my shortlist” / “Build my decision brief”. A mailto fallback must remain present even when the lead-form script is available. Only say “within one business day” / “一个工作日内回复”. Any free application assistance excludes school, government and third-party fees.

## Design boundaries

Do not use trophy imagery, rank badges, fake scores, fake acceptance data, partner-logo walls or generic campus photography as proof. The sample comparison score bars are explicitly illustrative and do not represent a live recommendation.
